# Grammar floor

The patterns stories are allowed to use. **Edit this file** as you learn more — it's the one dial that controls how hard stories are to read.

WaniKani teaches vocabulary and kanji and deliberately no grammar at all, which is why this file has to exist. Without it a "story built from your WaniKani words" would be built on sentence patterns you've never seen, and you'd know every word and still not be able to read it.

**Current level: N5 foundation.** Set 2026-09-24, unverified — adjust up or down after the first couple of stories.

## Allowed

### Sentence frames
- `X は Y です。` / `X は Y じゃないです。`
- `X は Y でした。` / `X は Y じゃなかったです。`
- `X が あります / います` — inanimate / animate existence

### Verbs
- `〜ます` / `〜ません` — present and future
- `〜ました` / `〜ませんでした` — past
- `〜ています` — ongoing action or resulting state

### Adjectives
- い-adjectives: attributive (`おおきい いえ`) and predicate (`いえは おおきいです`)
- な-adjectives: `きれいな はな` / `はなは きれいです`
- Past and negative forms of both

### Particles
`は` `が` `を` `に` `で` `と` `の` `も` `へ` `から` `まで`

### Connectives
- `そして` — and then
- `でも` — but (sentence-initial)
- `〜が、` — but (joining clauses)
- `〜から、` — because

### Demonstratives
`これ / それ / あれ / どれ` · `この / その / あの / どの` · `ここ / そこ / あそこ / どこ`

### Question words
`なに / なん` `だれ` `どこ` `いつ` `どう`

## Not yet — don't use in stories

- て-form for anything except 〜ています
- Plain / dictionary form (casual register)
- Potential, passive, causative
- Conditionals: `〜たら` `〜ば` `〜と`
- `〜たい` (want to), `〜ながら`, `〜ことができる`
- Relative clauses modifying nouns
- Counters beyond ひとつ / ふたつ and basic numbers
- Keigo of any kind

## Raising the floor

Move a pattern up when you can read it without stopping to work it out — not when you've merely met it. A floor set too high produces stories that are technically at your level and practically unreadable, which is the specific failure that makes people stop reading.

One or two patterns at a time. There's no rush; the vocabulary is doing the heavy lifting.
