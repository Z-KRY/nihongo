# Quick question

For a single question asked in passing. **Answer, capture, stop.** This is not a session and must not turn into one.

## Before answering

Read `me/profile.md` — rung, instruction language, script preference. Skim `me/progress.md` for what they already know, so the answer uses vocabulary they have rather than introducing three new words to explain one.

## Answer

Short. Usually two or three lines.

- Give the word or the rule, then **one** example sentence built from vocabulary they already know.
- Follow the script preference in the profile — furigana on any kanji if that's what it says.
- **Flag register unprompted** if the word has one: casual/blunt/intimate/keigo, or gendered usage. This is the highest-value thing this skill does, because a dictionary won't tell them and media will actively mislead them.
- If a word is ambiguous or has a common false-friend reading, say so in a line.

**Do not:** drill them, quiz them, offer a practice exercise, list related vocabulary they didn't ask for, or explain grammar three levels above where they are. They asked one question.

If the question genuinely needs a lesson to answer — a whole tense, a whole particle system — say so in a sentence and suggest `/nihongo`. Don't half-teach it here.

## Capture

Two writes, both silent. Never announce them.

1. **`me/srs/deck.md`** — add a card at `interval: 1, due: 1`, into the section it belongs in. Questions the learner asked are the highest-retention cards in the deck: they wanted the word at the moment they asked, which is exactly the condition that makes it stick. Never skip this.
2. **`me/questions.md`** — append one line: date, the question, the short answer.

Don't touch `me/progress.md` or the journal. This isn't a session and shouldn't appear in the session record.

## The feedback loop

`me/questions.md` is what makes this more than a dictionary. **A topic asked three or more times is a genuine gap**, not curiosity — the learner keeps needing something the syllabus hasn't given them.

When you notice a third occurrence, say so in one line ("that's the third time you've asked about counters — worth a proper lesson") and add it to "Next session focus" in `me/progress.md`. That's the one case where this skill is allowed to write to progress.

## Register

Match the response format rules in `CLAUDE.md`: no headings, no tables unless comparing two or three forms, no summary. A quick question gets a quick answer.
